## Phase 1: Get Selling FAST (Week 1) — zero/low cost
1. **WhatsApp Business Catalog** (you already have WhatsApp-sourced photos, so this is the fastest win):
   - Convert to WhatsApp Business app, build a Catalog with each jar as an item: photo, name, size (ml), price, MOQ if wholesale.
   - Pin the catalog link in your WhatsApp "About" and share via status.
2. **Instagram + Facebook Shop**:
   - Create a business Instagram page ("Crokary" or similar).
   - Post the existing videos as **Reels** (jars/bottles look great in short unboxing/showcase clips) and photos as carousel posts.
   - Enable Instagram/Facebook Shopping to tag products directly in posts.
3. **Google Business Profile** (if you have a shop/warehouse) — lets local buyers find you and message/call directly.

## Phase 2: Organize the Product Catalog (Week 1-2)
Current files are generically named (`WhatsApp Image...jpeg`). Before listing anywhere:
1. **Sort photos by product** into folders, e.g. `Image/Matki-Jar-500ml/`, `Image/Drynuts-Jar-1000ml/`, `Image/Oil-Bottle-XXml/`.
2. **Build a simple product sheet** (Excel/Google Sheet) with columns: SKU, Product Name, Capacity (ml), Material (glass), Lid type/color, Price (retail), Price (wholesale/bulk), Stock qty, Photo filename(s), Video filename(s).
3. **Improve photos where needed**: shoot on a plain white/light background, consistent lighting (a few current shots have shadows/reflections or clutter in the background — those work for a shelf overview but not for a clean product listing).
4. Trim the videos into 10-20 sec clips per product for reels/listings (turntable view of the jar, lid opening, size comparison).

## Phase 3: Choose Your Main Sales Channel(s) (Week 2-3)
Pick based on answers to Phase 0:

| Channel | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **IndiaMART / TradeIndia** | Wholesale/B2B glass jar buyers | Very common for jar/bottle manufacturers in India; free basic listing |
| **Amazon / Flipkart / Meesho** | Retail/B2C, pan-India reach | Need GST for Amazon/Flipkart; Meesho is easier to start without much registration |
| **Shopify / WooCommerce (own website)** | Full brand control, own domain | Paid (Shopify ~$/mo) but best for building "Crokary" as a brand |
| **Instagram/WhatsApp shop only** | Fastest, lowest cost | Good for starting; manual order-taking |

**Recommendation:** Start with WhatsApp Catalog + Instagram (Phase 1) immediately while wholesale-focused sellers should also list on **IndiaMART**, and retail-focused sellers should add **Meesho or Amazon** once packaging/shipping is sorted.

## Phase 4: Set Up the Store
- Write clear product titles: `"Glass Matki Jar 500ml with Airtight Lid – Storage Jar for Kitchen"`.
- Add descriptions covering: capacity, material, lid type (metal/black/gold), use-case (dry fruits, pickles, gifting, spices), dishwasher-safe if applicable.
- Set prices: unit price + bulk/wholesale price breaks (e.g., 1-11 pcs vs. 12+ carton rate) — common in this category.
- Define **shipping/packaging** for fragile glass: bubble wrap + sturdy carton; factor breakage cost into pricing or offer local-delivery-only initially to reduce risk.

## Phase 5: Marketing & Growth (Ongoing)
- Post regularly: new arrivals, festival-specific jars (gifting season), bulk-order discounts.
- Use the existing shelf/inventory photo as a "our range" post to show scale/variety.
- Ask happy customers for reviews/photos to build trust (important for a new online seller).
- Consider WhatsApp broadcast lists for repeat wholesale buyers.

## Phase 6: Operations
- Simple order log (Google Sheet or free tool) tracking: order, customer, items, payment status, shipping status.
- Decide payment methods: UPI/bank transfer (instant, no fees) to start; add COD/online gateway (Razorpay/PayU) once volume grows.
